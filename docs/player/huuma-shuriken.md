# Player skills: Huuma shuriken (A)

Replaces Katon on the A key. Branch `feature/player-skills`.

**How it plays**
1. Press A: a QTE of 2 keys (`Cast Inputs Huuma`). When the second one is pressed the ninja plays a quick throw (the Mixamo `Frisbee Throw` clip copied to `Animations/Ninja/Ninja Huuma Throw.anim`, 0.8 s at speed 1.2, with the animation event `SkillHuuma` at 0.35 s, where the hand is fully forward) and the shuriken is thrown on its own toward the mouse: the player turns to the mouse (`LookAtMouse`) and it flies straight along the floor plane (1.2 m high) up to 20 m (`Huuma Range`) at 40 m/s (`Huuma Speed`). No click is needed.
2. It stops at the first solid thing it touches (a sphere cast of 0.5 m radius against every layer, triggers ignored, so it also stops at the walls of Vexara arena, which are on the Ignore Raycast layer): walls, rocks, the boss. The player and their clones do not stop it. If it touches nothing it lands at 20 m.
3. When it stops it drops to the floor (the nearest NavMesh point) and stays there for 5 s (`Huuma Lifetime`), spinning slowly with a glow, and blinks during the last second. While it is there the **A icon is highlighted** (the same gold pulsing frame as the kick).
4. Pressing A while it is on the ground **swaps places**: the player is warped to the shuriken (the nearest NavMesh point to it, so a shuriken stuck in a wall puts the player next to the wall; `NavMeshAgent.Warp`, the current path is cleared), a purple flash (`OrbBounceEffect`) shows at both places, and the shuriken is used up. If nobody swaps, it just vanishes after 5 s.
5. The cooldown (`Huuma CD`, 8 s, a first guess) starts when the shuriken is gone, whether it timed out or was used for a swap. While the shuriken is out, A cannot throw another one. If the QTE is failed the cooldown starts right away, like the other skills.

**Pieces**
- `Characters/Ninja/HuumaShuriken.cs` (prefab `Prefabs/Ninja/HuumaShuriken`): flight, collision, waiting on the ground, lifetime. It tells the controller when it stuck and when it ended through two callbacks. The look is a flat quad (generated pinwheel texture `Materials/HuumaShuriken.png`, 1.8 m wide, four curved blades, a red ring around the hole) spinning on the vertical axis, a white-blue trail (`HuumaTrail`) while it flies and a point light.
- `NinjaController`: `Skill Huuma` fields, `SkillHuuma` (animation event), `OnHuumaStuck`, `OnHuumaEnded`, `SwapWithHuuma`, the A key branch, the cooldown, `Skill_Huuma` animator hash.
- Animator: trigger `Skill_Huuma`, state `Skill_Huuma` (tag `AnimationLock`), transition `Casting` to it when `Skill_Huuma` is set and `Casting` is false (0.1 s), exit to `Idle` at 0.9.
- HUD: `Spell Huuma` in the spell bar of both scenes (a copy of the kick icon in the slot after E, picture `iconHuuma.png`, generated: a steel shuriken on a dark blue background).

**Verified** (in play, in `VexaraScene`, simulating the end of the QTE because the CLI cannot press keys): a throw toward +x stopped at the east wall at x 16.3 (wall at about 16.9) and one toward -x at the west wall; the shuriken was stuck and the A icon highlighted; with no swap it vanished 5 s later with the highlight off; the swap moved the player from (-0.1, -10.3) to (-16.3, -4.4), where the shuriken had stuck, and removed the shuriken; the HUD shows Space, Q, W, E, A; 0 console errors. **Not verified:** the throw by hand with real keys and the mouse (the direction comes from the mouse ray), the throw animation timing (the release event is at 0.35 s of the frisbee clip, picked from the hand position), how the shuriken looks next to the ninja, Gorath scene (`TestScene`) and its rocks, the swap onto the boss or an odd spot, the failed QTE path.

**Choices that can change:** the swap consumes the shuriken (a swap back would need it to stay); the cooldown starts when it is gone; the throw has no damage yet (it is only for the swap; damaging bosses or counters can be added); the QTE is 2 keys with no click.
