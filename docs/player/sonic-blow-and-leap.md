# Player skills: Sonic Blow (F) and Leap (R)

Both are in `NinjaController` (`Sonic Blow` and `Leap` sections), with the tuning fields under `Skill Sonic Blow` and `Skill Leap` on the ninja prefab. Neither needs animation events: the timing is in code.

## Sonic Blow (F)

A flurry of fast strikes in a box in front of the ninja (`Length` x `Width`; 6 hits over 1.4 s: 5 normal strikes of 2 and a finisher of 5, see below), turning toward the mouse while it lasts. No immunity; getting hit, dashing (Space) or swapping with the Huuma shuriken cancels it.
- **Animation:** the `Skill_Whirlwind` state (the name is left from the old whirlwind) plays `Melee Punch`. Every strike crossfades into a new punch (`SonicBlowStrikeFade`) starting at `SonicBlowStrikeStart`, so the clip does not pop.
- **Root motion is turned off for the skill**: the punch clip rotates the root and spun the ninja away from the mouse.
- **Zigzag:** `OnAnimatorIK` moves the right hand between a low-left and a high-right point (`Swing Width/Low/High/Reach`), one strike at each end; the slash VFX alternate their tilt. This needs **IK Pass** on the Base Layer of the ninja controller.
- **Kunai:** `Kunai Held.prefab` (visual copy of the thrown kunai) is attached to the right hand bone while the skill lasts (`Kunai Position/Euler`, tuned for the IK hand goal).
- **VFX:** the Electro slash instances are destroyed when the skill stops.

## Leap (R)

Throws 5 `Shuriken Ground` at the mouse point (a shallow arc, 2 damage). A shuriken only hurts while it flies (anything within 0.7 m of it, once per target); the first hit destroys it like the other projectiles. If it hits nothing it sticks in the ground for 1.5 s as a harmless prop and then sinks: tested with a boss standing on three stuck shurikens, no damage. The throw range is 30 m. In flight hits make a throw at a spot beyond the boss, or at melee range, hit every time (tested: 5 of 5 at 1.8 m) and jumps backwards, away from the mouse.
- **Phases** (`LeapPhase`): Rise (0.4 s, the agent's base offset lifts the ninja and `agent.Move` pushes it back), Hang (slow motion, QTE), Fall (0.3 s).
- **Hang:** `Time.timeScale` goes to `Leap Slow Motion` (0.25) and the QTE of 3 random keys (never R) runs on unscaled time for `Leap Qte Time` (1 s). Each correct key throws a kunai at the mouse (`ShootBasicAttack` in `PlayerController`, 2 damage). A wrong key or running out of time only ends the shooting: no damage animation, the ninja just falls.
- **Animation:** base layer states `Skill_Leap`, `Leap_Fall` and `Leap_Throw`. `Skill_Leap` plays the `Ninja Dodge` backflip with its speed driven by the float `LeapSpeed` (`Leap Flip End` 0.64 x 2.15 s / `Leap Rise Time` 0.4 s), so the flip is over when the slow motion starts. At the start of the hang the trigger `Leap_Hang` blends into `Leap_Fall` (the library's looping `Fall Loop`: knees up, arms out), and during the QTE the ninja sinks slowly from `Leap Height` to `Leap Hang End Height` (0.8), like a slow fall; the Fall phase then drops her from wherever she is. She faces the mouse and leans back `Leap Hang Pitch` (20 degrees), applied to the hips bone in `LateUpdate`.
- **Bug fixed (leaning the root):** the first version tilted the root transform for the lean. With the NavMeshAgent and the base offset that made the agent shift her sideways every frame, and she ended up 35 m away (z -37) at the start of the hang. The lean is now only on the hips bone and the root keeps its yaw, so she stays within 0.5 m of her jump spot.
- **Safety:** `StopLeap` (called from `ResetPlayerState`) puts the time scale, base offset and root motion back, so a hit, dash or Huuma swap in the air cancels it cleanly.
- **Tested** by driving the methods from the CLI (it cannot press keys): the timeout path, the dash and swap cancels, the kunai throws, the shuriken landing and the QTE UI. The key presses themselves were not tested by hand.
- **Aiming:** the mouse point comes from `GetMouseAimPoint` (all layers, ignoring triggers), because both bosses are on the Ignore Raycast layer and the normal mouse ray passes through them and hits the ground behind. Tested by moving the real OS cursor over the game view (the game's mouse position is the screen cursor minus a fixed offset): aimed at the boss body all 5 shurikens hit, aimed at the ground 3.5 m beside it 3 of 5 hit.
- **Throw animation per kunai:** each kunai sets the trigger `Throw_Kunai`. The throw plays on a second animator layer `Throw Upper Body` (override, mask `Upper Body Mask`: everything except both legs and the Root, **default weight 0**), so the base layer keeps the tucked `Leap_Fall` legs. The layer plays `Ninja Kunai Throw` (a copy of the basic-attack `Ninja Shoot` clip with its `BasicAttack` event removed) at 2.2x; the code gives the layer weight 1 for `ThrowArmDuration` (0.36 s) and fades it out over the last 0.1 s (an idle layer left at full weight held the last pose before). The clip gets its forward reach from the pelvis, which the mask leaves out, so the right hand is pushed out toward the aim with IK (`ApplyThrowReachIk`, arm length measured on the rig, weight ramps up in 0.12 s and down in the last 0.14 s) and peaks right when the kunai leaves. Including Root in the mask made the arm reach by itself but swung the legs down about 0.25 m, so it was rejected. Measured: feet positions stay constant during a throw; the hand goes from 0.14 to 0.5 forward of the hips.
- **Cancels:** `StopLeap` (from `ResetPlayerState`) restores the time scale, base offset, root motion and clears the lean; the `Leap_Fall` state leaves when `Leaping` goes false.
- **Hang pose, release timing and speed (latest):** during the hang she sinks only from 2.2 to 2.05 (`Leap Hang End Height`, about 10% of the earlier 1.4 drop), so she stays in the sky while shooting. The Animator runs on `UnscaledTime` during the hang (restored in `RestoreLeapTime`), so she moves at normal speed inside the slow motion and a throw takes 0.27 s instead of over a second. A knee tuck is applied to the legs in `LateUpdate` (`Leap Tuck Thigh` 20, `Leap Tuck Knee` 20 degrees, eased in during the hang): bigger values (40/35) made her look seated. Each key plays the throw clip immediately and the kunai is released `LeapKunaiReleaseDelay` (0.17 s of real time) later, when the IK reach peaks. Serialized values live in the ninja prefab, so a change to a code default does nothing until the prefab value is changed too (this hid the tuck change once).

## Sonic Blow finisher (latest)

`Hits` is 6 and the last one is the finisher. The 5 normal strikes are evenly spaced (`GetSonicBlowInterval`), then there is a pause (`Finisher Pause`, 0.3 s) and the finisher lands (timeline measured: strikes at 0.13 s intervals from 0.1 s, finisher at 1.07 s, skill ends at 1.2 s). During the pause the right hand is pulled back and low (IK, "cocked") and then thrusts forward. The finisher does `Finisher Damage` (5, crits double it), reaches `Finisher Reach Scale` (1.4x) further and wider, spawns a straight slash at `Finisher Slash Scale` (2.2x) and zooms the camera in with `Cameras/CameraZoomPunch` (added to the main camera at runtime; `Finisher Zoom` 1.3: ortho size 12 to about 9.2 in 0.07 s, hold 0.15 s, back in 0.35 s, on real time and only touching the size while it runs, so it works with `CameraController` and `FixedArenaCamera`). No hit-stop was added. Verified with a boss in front: 5 normal hits then a hit of 5, and a dash during the skill cancels it with the camera back at 12.

## Leap kunai spawn and aim (latest)

Each kunai spawns at the right hand bone at the moment the arm is stretched (the basic attack spawns from a `ShootPosition` object in front of the ninja; the first version here used the chest and then a height clamp and looked wrong). The hand is above a boss' body (y of about 4.3 against a boss top of 4.1 and 3.6), so a perfectly level kunai flew over the boss: it flies level when the mouse is on the ground, and when the mouse is over something that can be hurt it tilts down just enough to hit that point (at most `LeapKunaiMaxPitch`, 20 degrees). The jump was lowered (`Leap Height` 1.9, `Leap Hang End Height` 1.75). Tested with the cursor on both bosses at 10 m and 24 m: 3 of 3 kunai hit each time. The ninja only turns to the mouse when a kunai is thrown, and the head is excluded from the throw mask.

## Latest tweaks (supersede the numbers above)

- **Leap height:** `Leap Height` is 0.9 and `Leap Hang End Height` 0.8 (they were 1.9 and 1.75, and before that 2.2 and 2.05). Measured hand height at the moment of a throw: 4.41 m at 1.9, 3.33 m at 1.0, 2.97 m at 0.7, against boss bodies that end at about 4.0 m (Gorath) and 3.6 m (Vexara), so a kunai thrown from the hand no longer flies over a boss. With the cursor on the boss, 3 of 3 kunai hit Gorath at 10 m and Vexara at 16 to 24 m (in slow motion a 20 m shot takes about 2 s of real time, so wait before counting hits).
- **Finisher as a charge:** the skill lasts 1.4 s and `Finisher Pause` is 0.5 s. From half an interval after the 5th normal strike (`GetSonicBlowChargeStart`) until the finisher the right hand winds up in a circle overhead and behind her: the angle accelerates (`charge^1.6`, 1.375 turns plus a 0.75 pi lead), the radius grows from 0.45 to 0.7 and a small tremble grows with the charge (`FinisherCharge*` constants); the finisher then thrusts forward from the top of the wind-up. The camera starts closing in slowly at the same moment (`Charge Zoom`, 1.12) and the finisher zoom (1.3) continues from that zoom without a jump (`CameraZoomPunch.Punch` starts from the zoom it has now). Measured timeline with a boss in front: 5 hits between 0.04 and 0.49 s, charge from 0.6 s with the camera going 12 to 10.7, finisher hit of 5 at 1.12 s with the camera at 9.2. Serialized values live in the prefab (duration, pause, heights were updated there too).
