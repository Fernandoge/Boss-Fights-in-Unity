# Ulvane (third boss): first build

Idea #1 in `docs/ideas.md`. Status: placeholder slice built and checked in Play mode through the CLI on 2026-10-10: the model, the forest level, one skill for Ulvane and one for each of the 7 animals. Everything is tuning-level placeholder; nothing here has been playtested by hand yet.

## Concept

A longbow hunter who keeps his distance and calls animals. Gorath is a brute that chases, Vexara is a stationary caster that teleports; Ulvane is a handler: the animals do most of the attacking and the fight is about reading which animal is coming and where. Every animal has exactly one skill.

## What exists

- **Model:** `Assets/Asset Packs/Ulvane/Model/Erika Archer.fbx` (the Mixamo Erika Archer with bow and arrow, moved from `docs/`). Set to Humanoid (own avatar), materials extracted to `Ulvane/Materials`, textures to `Ulvane/Textures`. The mesh names inside the FBX are shifted against their contents (the mesh called `Arrow_Mesh` is the clothes, `Eyes_Mesh` is the arrow); the materials follow the contents, so it renders right. The arrow is a separate skinned mesh on the `mixamorig:arrow` bone, so it can be hidden later.
- **Prefab:** `Prefabs/Characters/Bosses/Ulvane.prefab` (scale 2.25; 2.6 matched Gorath 4.76 and Vexara 4.70 (4.7 units tall), 1.95 was tried, 2.25 is about 4.1 units; arrow height 2.25 and forward offset 1.4 follow the scale; NavMeshAgent speed 3.2, Base Offset -0.05 (the soles sat 0.15 above the floor at scale 2.6; the offset scales with the transform, so this is the same fix at 2.25; not re-measured after the size change), the mesh `Erika_Archer_Eyes_Mesh` is switched off (the FBX mesh names are shuffled against the materials: this one is really the arrow, which hung at her feet), capsule collider on the root, layer Ignore Raycast, 300 health). Animator: `Animations/Ulvane/Ulvane Controller.controller`, built only from library clips, see `docs/ulvane/animations.md`.
- **Scripts** (`Scripts/Bosses/Ulvane/`, namespace `Bosses.Ulvane`):
  - `UlvaneBoss`: kiting movement, attack choice (never the same twice), the arrow shot, the beast sequence, a placeholder phase 2.
  - `UlvaneBeast` (abstract): the animal lifecycle (pop in beside Ulvane or at the edge, perform its skill, pop out), movement that stops at the NavMesh edge, telegraph helpers. One subclass per animal.
  - `UlvaneProjectile`: arrows and snowballs. Straight over the floor, stops at the NavMesh edge.
  - `UlvaneAttack`: the enum the harness uses.
  - Shared: `Shared/GroundBlast` (a circle that fills, then hurts inside it), `Shared/NavMeshUtility` (snap to the floor, clamp a line to the edge).
- **Beast prefabs:** `Prefabs/Characters/Bosses/Ulvane/<Animal> Beast.prefab`, variants of the `Animals_FREE` prefabs with the pack's movement scripts removed. Scales follow real size with the ninja (2.04 units tall) as 1.7 m, so 1 m is about 1.2 units, measured on baked meshes on 2026-10-12: tiger 1.2 (1.5 u tall), deer 1.6 (3.4 u with antlers), horse 1.83 (2.5 u), dog 1.4 (1.0 u), chicken 2.0 (0.86 u), penguin 1.0 (1.35 u), kitty 2.5 (0.77 u). Skill radii are serialized and do not depend on scale. The animals only have idle, walk and run clips, so every skill is code driven.
- **Level:** `Scenes/UlvaneScene` (in Build Settings), see below.
- **Shared change:** `BossController` now flashes every material of the model (hit red, counter green), not just the first one, because Ulvane is built from 6 meshes. Gorath and Vexara have one material each, so they look the same.
- **Debug harness:** works for Ulvane. Keys 1-8 force an attack in `UlvaneAttack` order, F5 repeats the one chosen in `Ulvane Attack To Use` on the `DebugHarness` object.

## Skills

| Key | Attack | Who | What happens | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ArrowShot | Ulvane | Draws the bow, an orange lane locks on the player (aim with the perceived position plus a little lead), arrow flies down the lane. Phase 2: 3 arrows in a fan. | 2 |
| 2 | TigerPounce | Tiger | Crouches while a circle fills where the player is heading, leaps and lands on it. | 2 |
| 3 | DogWhirlwind | Dog | Chases its tail in a tightening circle for 1.6 s while a red circle fills around it, then becomes a whirlwind (Taz style) that hunts the player for 6 s, turning slowly. The circle stays under it and is the damage area. | 1 per 0.7 s inside |
| 4 | HorseTrample | Horse | Appears at the edge, a wide lane fills towards the player, gallops down the whole lane. | 2 |
| 5 | DeerGore | Deer | Stops about 12 units from the player and a huge cone (15 long, 50 degrees) fills in the direction it faces, locked at the start of the 1.5 s telegraph; it lunges 1.5 and hits everything in the cone. Four casts in a row, re-aimed each time. While a cone fills an F prompt floats above it: a kick or counter (`ICounterable`) cancels the cone and the whole skill and stuns the deer for 3 s (it wobbles). | 1 per cast |
| 6 | ChickenEggs | Chicken | Runs circles around the player laying 5 eggs, each a circle that explodes 1.3 s later. | 1 |
| 7 | PenguinSnowballs | Penguin | 5 lanes fill in a fan, a snowball flies down each. | 1 |
| 8 | KittyPetOrClaw | Kitty | Runs up and flops on its back with a T prompt above it (same look as the F counter prompt). T within 7 units pets it: hearts, it purrs and the skill ends. Ignore it for 3 s and it gets up, swells to 5x and rakes three claw lanes (fan of 24 degrees, 0.8 s warning). | 2 |

All the animals aim with `PerceivedPlayerPosition` / `PerceivedPlayerVelocity` (through `UlvaneBoss.PlayerAimPoint`), never the live position. Skills of 2 hearts show the danger icon above Ulvane.

Kiting: he stays 8 to 13 units from the player (backs away when closer, walks closer when farther, circles in between at 60% speed, switches side every 2 to 4 s, slides sideways if cornered). His speed (3.2) is below the player's run speed, so a melee player can catch him. While a skill runs he stands still and faces the player.

## Level

`Scenes/UlvaneScene` is a copy of `TestScene` with the same follow-the-player orthographic camera (pitch 30, yaw 45, size 12), the player, the UI and the harness. Gorath and Vexara are removed; `BossSelector` has one entry (Ulvane).

- **Open arena:** a 51 x 51 flat floor with nothing on it, so movement is free and only the mechanics matter. Checked: 784 straight lines between points all over the arena, none touches an edge. Decoration inside is flat only (dirt patches, grass tufts, no colliders).
- **Forest wall:** the outer ring is dense trees (the Fantasy Forest pack's `tree_1`) and a few rocks. It is made not walkable with four `NavMeshModifierVolume` walls, so the wall holds even where the trees leave gaps. The rocks (RockFREE) are about 20 times too big at scale 1, so they are scaled to 3.5 to 6.5 units.
- **NavMesh:** a `NavMeshSurface` on `Forest Level` built from physics colliders; data in `Scenes/UlvaneScene/NavMesh-Forest.asset`. Rebuild it after moving trees or walls (`NavMeshSurface.BuildNavMesh()`, then save the data as an asset; the CLI's `bake_navmesh_surfaces` is not implemented for surfaces).
- **Trees hide when in the way:** `Cameras/ForestOcclusionFader` (on the camera) casts from the camera to the player and the boss, and trees (`Cameras/ForestTree`) in the way stop being drawn but keep their shadow. They pop instead of fading.
- Fog, a procedural sky and a warm sun set the mood. Ground is the pack's `grass01` texture on a Standard material.

## Patrolling animals (2026-10-12, branch `feature/ulvane-patrol`)

Design change: the animals are no longer summoned. All 7 are in the arena from the first frame and patrol it (walk to a random walkable point, stand 1.5 to 4 s, repeat; the point is picked by area from the NavMesh triangulation, away from the player at spawn and outside other animals' zones). When Ulvane picks an attack he gestures, the animal drops its patrol, runs `Approach()` (gets to where its skill starts: horse and kitty gallop to a flank point, tiger and chicken run to the player's range, the others start where they stand), does `Perform()`, then goes back to patrolling. Ulvane still waits for the animal to finish, so only one animal works at a time (the old idea for this was removed from `docs/ideas.md`).

- Code: `UlvaneBeast` (`Spawn`, `Command`, `IsFinished`, `IsAvailable`, patrol fields under `Patrol`), `UlvaneBoss.SpawnBeasts` and `GetPatrolPoint`, new `UlvanePatrolZone`.
- Penguin ice zone: the object `Penguin Ice Zone` in `UlvaneScene` (centre 12, 8, radius 5) has an `UlvanePatrolZone` for `PenguinSnowballs`; the penguin only patrols inside it and fires from it. The floor is two flat discs with no collider (`Assets/Materials/IceFloor.mat`, `IceFloorRim.mat`), so the arena stays open and the NavMesh is untouched. Other animals avoid walking points inside the zone. No gameplay effect yet: it is only the penguin's home (idea: ice makes the player slide).
- No contact damage from patrolling animals; only the skills hurt.
- Checked in Play mode: all 7 patrol (positions change over time), the penguin stays on the ice, the horse gallops to a flank point and then fires its lane, the 7 forced attacks ran with 0 console errors. Not checked: each skill start from every possible position (the tiger leap and dog hunt from far away), animals stuck against trees (a point that cannot be reached times out after 15 s), foot sliding against `Patrol Speed` 2.5 (penguin 1.4), how it feels with auto attacks on.

**Patrol areas (2026-10-12, same branch):** each beast prefab has `Patrol Area` (`UlvanePatrolArea`): Horse, Tiger and Deer are `NearWalls` (walkable points 1.5 to 8 m from the nearest NavMesh edge, picked in steps of at most 14 m so they follow the walls instead of cutting across the middle), Kitty, Chicken and Dog are `Center` (at least 12 m from the walls), and the Penguin stays in its zone. The numbers are `Wall Band Distance`, `Min Wall Clearance`, `Wall Patrol Step` and `Center Min Wall Distance` on the boss. Skills still move the animals wherever they need (the approach runs to the player or a flank point), so a wall animal leaves its band for a skill and walks back afterwards. `UlvanePatrolZone` radius now follows the object's scale (the zone root is scaled 0.66 in the scene, so the circle is about 3.3 m). Checked in Play mode by sampling positions over time: the penguin stayed within 2 m of the zone centre, the centre animals 12 m or more from the walls, the wall animals mostly 2 to 9 m from them (a few samples further out right after a skill).

## Animals act on their own (2026-10-12, branch `feature/ulvane-patrol`, old idea, since removed from `docs/ideas.md`)

The fight is meant to be chaotic, so Ulvane no longer commands the animals. Each `UlvaneBeast` has its own `Cooldown` and `First Use Delay` (header `Cooldown` on each `<Animal> Beast.prefab`). The cooldown runs while the animal patrols; when it hits 0 the animal drops its patrol, runs `Approach()` and `Perform()` by itself, and the cooldown restarts when the skill ends. The first use is `First Use Delay` (3 s) plus a random 0 to one cooldown, so they do not all fire at the start. Cooldowns (all guesses): kitty 7, penguin 8, tiger 9, deer 10, chicken 12, dog 14, horse 15.

- Ulvane's own loop (`timeBetweenAttacks`) now only picks the Arrow Shot. `BeastSequence` stays as the future "special call": it gestures and `Command()`s a beast, skips the cooldown wait, and only works when that beast is free (`IsAvailable`), so a call never interrupts a skill already running. Nothing picks it yet except the debug harness (keys 2-8, F5).
- `UlvaneBoss.CanBeastsAct` holds the animals back (they finish the skill they are in) while the boss is dead, roaring into phase 2, the player is dead, or the harness switched auto attacks off (F1).
- Not done: the danger icon (2-heart skills) used to show over Ulvane; animals have no collider to hang it on, so autonomous skills show none for now. Ulvane's attack pace (`Time Between Attacks` 3.5 s on the prefab, 3 s in phase 2) was tuned when he chose animals too; with only the arrow it may be too slow.
- Checked in Play mode through the CLI (player invulnerable, 3x speed, 340 game seconds): all 7 fire repeatedly on their own, two or three often work at once, 0 console errors. Not checked: the feel of it by hand, the first-use stagger after the last change (compiled, not re-run), phase 2 with the roar.

## Phase 3: the last stand (2026-10-12, branch `feature/ulvane-patrol`)

At 0 health Ulvane falls and stays down (`UlvaneBoss.OnHealthDepleted` returns true, `isImmuneToDamage`, `LastStandSequence`). The player has to survive `Phase 3 Duration` (20 s) while a "SURVIVE!" banner shows (`UI/SurviveBanner`). The animals are enraged (`UlvaneBeast.Enrage`: `Enraged Cooldown Scale` 0.28, `Enraged Animation Speed` 1.3, red `Enraged Tint`), so they use their skills about three or four times as often and nobody is safe. When the time is up they finish the skill they are in and `Mourn`: the red tint is restored, each follows a NavMesh path to its own place on a circle of `Mourn Radius` 4.5 around his body, turns to face him and stands there with a sad face floating above it (`UI/SadFace`, drawn in code, placed on a trigger as tall as the animal). Once all are in place (or after `Mourn Arrive Timeout` 25 s) and `Mourn Time` 3 s passed, `Defeat(false)` shows the victory screen after `Victory Screen Delay` (4 s), with the animals still standing there. `OnDefeated` leaves the animals alive after a last stand. For a test from the CLI: `UnityEngine.Object.FindFirstObjectByType<Bosses.Ulvane.UlvaneBoss>().TakeDamage(100000, false);`, with `set_timescale 8` to skip the 20 s (the harness F7 plus one hit also works). Not checked: the feel and the difficulty of the 20 s, the tint colours coming back, and the player dying during it.

**Pet moment, hearts (2026-10-12):** `Love hit` loops every 1 s and bursts once per loop, so 1.4 s of emitting gave only two bursts. `Pet Moment Time` is 3.2 s and `Pet Effect Stop Early` 1 s now (prefab and code defaults), so the hearts emit for 2.2 s (bursts at 0, 1 and 2 s) and the last ones still have a second to float before the player gets up. Not watched at normal speed.


## Calmer fight: animals take turns, no cooldowns (2026-10-14)

The fight was too chaotic and Ulvane got lost in it, so only the last stand (phase 3) keeps the chaos. The per-animal cooldowns are gone (`Cooldown`, `First Use Delay`, `Enraged Cooldown` removed from the code and the beast prefabs); Ulvane's turn order sends the animals instead.

- **Turn order:** `UlvaneBoss.BeastTurns` runs for the whole fight. After `First Beast Delay` (4 s), whenever fewer than the allowed number of animals are working and `Time Between Beast Starts` (3.5 s) passed since the last start, it sends the next animal of a shuffled bag (`Command()`, no gesture). Every animal goes once before any goes twice, and the one that just went is not first in the next bag. Phase 1: `Max Beasts At Once` 2. Phase 2: `Phase Two Max Beasts At Once` 2 (the Snow Tornado combo holds the dog and the penguin, so a third can start while it runs). Phase 3: no limit, one new start every `Last Stand Time Between Beast Starts` (0.9 s), so all seven end up working together, staggered. Ulvane's own Arrow Shot loop is unchanged. Special calls by `BeastSequence` still work.
- **Horse:** the charge lane now ends a little past the player (`Overshoot Distance` 10, capped by `Max Lane Length` and the edge) instead of crossing the whole arena (it ran 42 m to the far wall and back, then galloped home at 7 u/s).
