# Second boss: Timed Explosions

The fifth attack of the wizard (after Spell Circles, Diagonal Lines, Orb Barrage and Starfall). A memory test like the clock waves, but with positions on the floor instead of numbers. First version, meant to be iterated after playtesting. Branch `feature/timed-explosions`.

**What it does:** the boss raises both hands (`PlayCastArea`, same animation as Starfall and the clock waves). The arena is split into 3 equal zones, either 3 vertical strips (left, center, right) or 3 horizontal bands (bottom, middle, top), picked at random (`Zone Vertical Chance` 0.5), in a random order. The zones light up one after the other in pale blue, 0.5 s each with 0.2 s between them (1.9 s in total) and go dark. After a pause of 1.2 s they explode in the same order they lit up, one every 1.3 s, with no warning: each explosion is a red flash over the whole zone for 0.45 s and hurts for 1 if the player is inside it at that moment. Because the zones cover the whole arena, the player has to be in one of the other two zones at every explosion, so they must remember the order. The boss teleports 0.3 s after the lights go out (it does not stay in the zone it is standing in). The attack ends one `timeBetweenAttacks` (0.35 s) before the first explosion, so the next attack starts casting just as the first explosion goes off, while the other two are still to come (measured: the boss was free 0.35 s before the first explosion; the zones keep running by themselves). The zones keep running into whatever attack or clock intermission comes next, nothing clears them.

**Pieces**
- `Bosses/Second Boss/TimedExplosionZone.cs` (prefab `TimedExplosionZone`): one zone. It is a flat quad lying on the floor (material `ZoneGlow`, additive, texture `ZoneGlow.png`: a thin bright border and a soft fill) that owns its own timeline (`Begin` gets the light start, the light time and the explosion time, all counted from the moment the attack spawns it), so the whole attack is three self-contained objects. It fades in and out for the light, flashes for the explosion and destroys itself.
- `SecondBoss.cs`: `Timed Explosions` header (`Zone Prefab`, `Zone Cast Delay` 0.5, `Zone Light Time` 0.5, `Zone Light Gap` 0.2, `Zone Pause Time` 1.2, `Zone Explosion Interval` 1.3, `Zone Teleport Delay` 0.3, `Zone Damage` 1, `Zone Vertical Chance` 0.5), `TimedExplosionsSequence`, `GetExplosionZones`; `SecondBossAttack.TimedExplosions` makes it part of the random attack pool (never twice in a row) and of the clock mechanic like any attack.
- Harness with the second boss: key 8 forces it (`DebugForceTimedExplosions`); F5 can repeat it alone through `Attack To Use`.

**Colours:** light is `(0.3, 0.5, 0.8, 0.4)` and explosion `(0.9, 0.28, 0.12, 0.7)` on the prefab. The material is additive, so the first values (a bright cyan-white and orange-white) washed out to solid white and were toned down.

**Verified:** compiles with 0 console errors; forced in play, three zones appear in a random order, each lights up once in sequence and then explodes in the same order (left/right/center, lights at 0, 0.7 and 1.4 s, explosions at 3.1, 4.4 and 5.7 s in one run), a horizontal band was seen lit in blue and then exploding in red, and a player standing in an exploding zone lost 1 heart. **Not verified yet:** how it feels by hand (the hit lock of about 1.4 s and the 2.2 s hit immunity mean a player hit by one zone is immune to the next one, 1.3 s later), whether 0.5 s of light is readable enough, the look on a real screen, a natural run mixed with the other attacks and the clock mechanic.

**Known limits / ideas:** no sound and no particles (the explosion is a flat red flash, a pack effect could be added over each zone); the lights and explosions ignore the boss and the orbs; no phase 2 variant (4 or 5 zones, a repeated sequence); the zones are always thirds of the whole arena, never squares or other shapes.
