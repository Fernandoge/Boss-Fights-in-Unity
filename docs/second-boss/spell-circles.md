# Second boss: Spell Circles and Teleport

(The boss's second skill, Diagonal Lines, is in `diagonal-lines.md`; between attacks it picks one of the two, never the same one twice in a row.)

The second boss ("SecondBoss", placeholder name) is a wizard that never walks. It stands still, faces the player, and repeats one cycle: cast Spell Circles, then teleport to another spot.

**Spell Circles:** the boss spawns 7 small circles, one every 0.42 s (Karthus Q style), restarting a hand-only cast animation for each one (the circle appears 0.28 s after the animation restarts, when the hand reaches its peak). Each circle is a `SkillIndicator` circle (radius 1.2 m) whose fill grows from the centre over 0.5 s; when the fill reaches the rim it explodes (`SpellCircleImpact` prefab: a copy of `SmallExplosion`, scaled to 0.4 so it fits the circle, parts scaled together, fireball and embers lifted about 0.9 m so the floor does not cut them, shockwave laid flat on the ground, played 1.4x faster) and hurts the player once if they are inside it (1 damage; the player's own damage immunity applies). Each circle picks its position separately:
- 50% "lead": where the player is heading (their NavMeshAgent velocity times the telegraph time) plus up to 0.8 m of random offset.
- 50% "offset": the player's current position plus a random offset of 0.5 to 2.5 m. A player who stands still is only safe if the offset happens to miss them.
Positions are snapped to the NavMesh so circles never appear inside walls.

**Reaction to fast moves (aim delay):** the circles (and the boss's facing) use `PerceivedPlayerPosition`, never `player.position`. It lives in `BossController` (`Player Perception` fields): when the player moves faster than they can run (more than `Fast Move Speed Factor` 1.3 times the player's NavMeshAgent speed, which catches the space-bar dash, the R leap, the E flip kick and the Huuma swap) the boss keeps aiming at the old spot for `Fast Move Reaction Delay` (0.8 s after the move ends) before it notices the new one, and it does not lead the player's movement during that time (`PerceivedPlayerVelocity` is zero). This replaced the old per-frame "jump detect distance" check, which missed the leap (about 0.3 m per frame) so circles snapped to the player at once and led them by 8 m. **Rule for every boss skill: aim with `PerceivedPlayerPosition` / `PerceivedPlayerVelocity`, so no movement skill of the player can be tracked instantly.**

**Teleport:** after the last circle lands the boss is squeezed into a thin, tall line (width 2% of normal, height 130%, 0.25 s, immune to damage meanwhile), warps to a random NavMesh spot 10 to 22 m from the player (8 to 20 m in the second level), at least 8 m from where it stood and at least 3 m from the nearest wall, faces the player and springs back out of the line (0.25 s). Then it waits `timeBetweenAttacks` (0.35 s) and casts again, so the fight is nearly continuous.

**Pieces**
- `Bosses/Second Boss/SecondBoss.cs`: the boss. Overrides `IdleMovement` (face the player only, never chase), `PerformAttack` (starts the cast sequence coroutine) and `EnterSecondPhase` (does nothing: no phase 2 yet, and the base version would freeze the boss). The whole sequence is timed in code, not by animation events, so the attack ends by itself (no `StopPerformingAttack` event needed). `DebugForceSpellCircles()` and `DebugTeleport()` are for the harness.
- `Bosses/Second Boss/SpellCircle.cs`: one circle. Owns its telegraph timer, the position-check damage and its own lifetime. Prefab `SpellCircle`, indicator prefab `SpellCircleIndicator` (copy of the line indicator with a 0.25 s hold after filling).
- `Shared/SkillIndicator.cs` `ShowCircle` plus pattern 2 in `Shaders/SkillIndicator.shader`.
- `BossController.IdleMovement` became `protected virtual` (the only change to shared boss code; boss 1 behaves the same).
- Animator `Second Boss Controller` (in `Assets/Animations/Second Boss/`): base layer `Idle` (default, looping, speed 1.25), and a second layer `Cast Hand` masked to the right arm and fingers (`Right Arm Mask`), so the legs and torso keep idling while only the arm casts. Its `Cast` state (speed 2) is reached from any state by trigger `Cast`, also from itself so it restarts for every circle, and returns to `Empty` at 85%, plus the base-class parameters (`Walking`, `PerformingAction`, `EnterSecondPhase`, `Countered`) so the base class never hits a missing parameter. Placeholder clips: `standing idle` and `standing 1H cast spell 01` (picked because it has the least body movement of the cast clips; the right arm peaks about 0.7 s into it). To try another cast clip, switch it to Humanoid first and drop it into the `Cast` state of the `Cast Hand` layer.
- Prefab `Prefabs/Characters/Bosses/Second Boss.prefab`: scale 2, NavMeshAgent copied from boss 1 (only used for `Warp` and NavMesh queries), capsule collider on the root, layer Ignore Raycast. The scene instance sets the shared health text reference.
- `BossSelector` in `TestScene` has the entries First Boss and Second Boss; set `Starting Boss Index` to 1 to fight the second boss.
- Harness with the second boss active: key 1 forces Spell Circles, key 2 forces a Teleport, F1, F2, F4 work as for boss 1.

**Model and textures:** `Nightshade J Friedrich` (Mixamo). The FBX embeds its textures but Unity did not extract them, so the four PNGs were extracted into `Asset Packs/Second Boss/Textures/` and a "Standard (Specular setup)" material `Nightshade.mat` is remapped onto the model in its importer (diffuse, normal, specular and glow maps; the specular map is what gives the gold trim and shine of the Mixamo preview).

**Colours:** the Mixamo textures were recoloured with a hue shift (originals are kept next to the `_purple` copies): gold and green both became purple, which leaves the black cloth and armour as the base (an earlier version with dark red trim and dark purple cloth is in commit 5497e78). The material uses `Nightshade_diffuse_purple`, `Nightshade_specular_purple` and `Nightshade_glow_purple`; the specular map matters most, because it carries the coloured shine. In the second level the purple accents were then desaturated to silver (`Nightshade_diffuse_silver`, `Nightshade_specular_silver`, which the material now uses) because a purple boss blended into the purple floor; the purple textures are still in the folder. The boss's brightness was matched to the player's by measuring the average rendered luminance of both in the level (about 53 each): the main control is the `Boss Glow` light intensity (0.25), since with the specular map the `_Color` and `_SpecColor` fields move it very little. The recolour was scripted (Pillow, hue range 5-85 degrees for gold and 78-205 for green, feathered, only for saturated pixels), so it can be redone with other colours.

**Animation import note:** Mixamo FBXs import as Generic. Only the model and the six clips in use were switched to Humanoid (animation type Humanoid, avatar copied from the model, `standing idle` set to loop). Switch any other clip the same way before using it.

**Starting values (tune in playtest):** 7 circles, radius 1.2, interval 0.42 s, telegraph 0.5 s, damage 1, 50% lead chance, offset 0.5 to 2.5 m, next attack 0.35 s after the teleport, 0.2 s pause after the last circle before vanishing, health 100.

**Known limits:** the cast animation is a placeholder and its release moment is only approximately synced to the circles; the impact effect is a copy of an existing explosion; no teleport VFX (it only squeezes into a line); no phase 2; no death handling; no sound.
