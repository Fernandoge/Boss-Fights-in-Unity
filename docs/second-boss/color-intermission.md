# Second boss: Color intermission (before phase 2)

The intermission that plays when the boss drops to half health, before the (not yet built) second phase. A memory test on the squares of the floor. First version, meant to be iterated after playtesting. Branch `feature/color-intermission`.

**When it starts:** `BossController.TakeDamage` starts `EnterSecondPhase` when health reaches half. `SecondBoss.EnterSecondPhase` sets the second phase flag (`hasEnteredSecondPhase`, made `protected` in the base class for this), waits until the boss is not in the middle of an attack and runs `ColorIntermissionSequence`. The clock mechanic is reset when it starts (no clock cycle, numbers or counters carry on). There is no second phase after it yet: when it ends the boss goes back to its normal attacks. Harness key 9 forces it at any health.

**What happens**
1. The boss teleports to the middle of the arena and is immune to damage for the whole intermission.
2. A 5 s warning (`Color Prelude Time`): everything still on the field from earlier attacks is removed (bouncing orbs fade, falling stars, lasers and explosion zones disappear, `ClearLingeringHazards`), the boss drops into a guard pose (Mixamo `Standing Block Start` then the looping `Standing Block Idle`, crouched with the arms crossed in front, which also reads as "immune") and stays in it for the whole intermission, and an orb of light above its head (`IntermissionCharge` prefab, the `ColorOrb` sprite) grows and cycles through the four colors faster and faster, then swells and fades just as the colors appear. It replaced a big star on the floor around the boss's feet and the repeated area cast animation, which felt overused.
3. The squares of the floor grid (the faint low-opacity lines of the floor shader, 4 m, which gives 8 x 8 squares; the border squares reach the arena edge) fade in, each with one of 4 colors (red, blue, green, yellow, an equal number of each, 16 squares per color, in random places). The colors are shown for 3 s (`Color Show Time`, it was 5 s) and fade out.
4. After 0.5 s, 6 rounds, one after the other. A round: a big disc of one color floats above the boss's head (it keeps the guard pose) (`ColorIcon` prefab, the same popup as the clock icon); the player has 1.6 s (`Color Run Time`, it was 3 s and then 2 s) to be standing on a square of that color; then every square of another color explodes (orange-red flash, 0.6 s) and a player standing on one takes 1 damage. The squares of the asked color glow in their color for 0.6 s so the right answer can be learned. 0.7 s later the next round starts.
5. **Color reset after round 4** (`Color Reset After Round`): the colors are thrown away. The boss casts with raised hands (`PlayCastArea`) and every square flickers through random colors for 1 s (`Color Shuffle Time`, `ColorSquareBoard.Reshuffle`), then flashes white and settles on a new random arrangement (again 16 squares per color). The new colors stay lit for the same 3 s as the first time (`Color Show Time`), fade out, the boss goes back to the guard pose and, after 0.5 s, rounds 5 and 6 are asked against the new board.
6. The 6 rounds use every color at least once and 2 repeated ones (4 colors, 6 rounds), in random order. The colors are shown only at the start and after the reset, so the player has to remember the whole board each time for about 40 s.
7. When the rounds end the boss teleports away and goes back to attacking.

**Animations:** the two clips are in `Asset Packs/Second Boss/Animations`, set to Humanoid with the boss avatar copied (`Block Idle` with Loop Time on) and added to the Second Boss Controller as the states `Block Start` (leads to `Block Idle` through exit time) and `Block Idle`; the script cross-fades into `Block Start` and the end-of-intermission teleport cross-fades back to `Idle`. Other long clips looked at and not used: `Standing 2H Magic Attack 03` (4.3 s, a forward lunge with the arms stretched, like a blast), `04`, `05` (a crouch and slam), `Standing Idle 03` (11.4 s idle).

**Pieces**
- `Bosses/Second Boss/ColorSquareBoard.cs` (prefab `ColorSquareBoard`, squares from prefab `ColorSquareCell`, a quad with the `ZoneGlow` material): builds the squares from the arena rectangle and the floor grid, owns the colors, fades them (`ShowColors`, `HideColors`), runs a round's explosion and damage check (`Explode`, `GetColorAt`) and removes itself (`Finish`).
- `Bosses/Second Boss/ClockNumberPopup.cs`: got `SetIconColor`, used to tint the disc (sprite `Materials/ColorOrb.png`).
- `SecondBoss.cs`: `Color Intermission` header (`Color Board Prefab`, `Color Icon Prefab`, `Intermission Colors`, `Color Rounds` 6, `Color Prelude Time` 5, `Color Charge Prefab`, `Color Show Time` 3, `Color Fade Time` 0.35, `Color Pause Time` 0.5, `Color Run Time` 1.6, `Color Reset After Round` 4, `Color Shuffle Time` 1, `Color After Explosion Time` 0.7, `Color Reveal Time` 0.6, `Color Damage` 1), `EnterSecondPhase`, `ColorIntermissionSequence`, `GetColorRounds`, `ResetClockMechanic`, `MarkBusy`.

**Verified:** compiles with 0 console errors; forced in play, the 8 x 8 colored squares line up with the floor grid and fade in, the colored disc shows above the boss in each round (blue, green...), the sequence ends by itself and the board is removed; an explosion for a color other than the player's square cost 1 heart and one for the player's own color cost nothing; the explosion shows the other squares in orange-red and the right ones in their color; taking the boss below half health through `TakeDamage` started the intermission by itself (board spawned, boss busy and immune). **Not verified yet:** how it feels by hand (1.6 s to run to a square of the right color, 1 damage per mistake, about 32 s in total with the reset), whether the saturated colors are too bright on a real screen, whether 5 s of warning is the right amount, whether 4 colors with an equal count is hard enough, colors for color-blind players (no symbol on the squares).

**Known limits / ideas:** no sound; the squares have no symbols or patterns besides color; no death handling yet, so failing every round only takes the player to zero or below; no second phase after it; the boss does not move during the rounds.
