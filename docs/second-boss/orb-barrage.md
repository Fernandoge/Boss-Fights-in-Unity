# Second boss: Orb Barrage

The third attack of the wizard (after Spell Circles and Diagonal Lines). First version, meant to be iterated after playtesting.

**What it does:** the boss throws 3 medium orbs (radius 0.8 m, about the size of the first boss's rocks) one after the other, 0.7 s apart, each with the one-hand cast animation. The throw angles are spread evenly across a 25 degree cone each side of the player and shuffled, so one dodge does not beat all three. After the last throw the boss waits 0.5 s and teleports like after the other attacks, while the orbs keep going. Each orb slides over the floor at 12.5 m/s, bounces off the four arena walls and off the other orbs, hurts the player for 1 on contact (the player's own hit immunity stops repeated hits) and fades after 9 s. Every bounce (wall or orb) gives the orb a new random speed of its launch speed plus 45-65% (`Bounce Speed Bonus` on the orb prefab), so orbs fly at 18.1-20.6 m/s after the first bounce. The bonus is not cumulative: it is always taken from the launch speed, so orbs never run away. An orb is not removed when it hits the player.

**Pieces**
- `Bosses/Second Boss/BouncingOrb.cs` (prefab `BouncingOrb`): one orb. Position-based, no physics: the wall bounce clamps the centre to the arena bounds and flips the velocity component; the orb-orb bounce swaps the velocity along the line between the centres (equal masses) and then restores each orb's speed, then pushes them apart. A static list of live orbs is how orbs find each other; each pair is handled once (by the orb with the lower instance id). Damage check is a flat distance test against the player's position (`radius + Player Hit Padding`, like the spell circle). Orbs grow in and drop from the boss's hand height (`Spawn Height`) to `Float Height` over `Grow Time`, and shrink over `Fade Time` at the end; they are not dangerous while growing or fading. `DespawnAll()` fades every live orb.
- Prefab `BouncingOrb`: root with the script, `Visual` (moved and scaled by the script) holding a sphere `Model` (material `OrbCore`, emissive purple), a point light `Glow` and a `TrailRenderer` (material `OrbTrail`, additive). The ground footprint is `OrbMarkerIndicator` (a copy of `SpellCircleIndicator` with the purple material `OrbMarker`), a `SkillIndicator` circle that stays and is moved by the orb every frame so the hit area can be read on the tilted camera.
- `SecondBoss.cs`: `Orb Barrage` header (`Orb Prefab`, `Orb Count` 3, `Orb Speed` 12.5, `Orb Lifetime` 9, `Orb Damage` 1, `Orb Release Delay` 0.28, `Orb Throw Interval` 0.7, `Orb After Throw Time` 0.5, `Orb Aim Spread` 25, `Orb Spawn Distance` 1.5, `Orb Wall Inset` 0.2), `OrbBarrageSequence`, `GetOrbAimAngles`, `SpawnOrb`, `GetOrbBounds`. `SecondBossAttack.OrbBarrage` makes it part of the random attack pool (never twice in a row) and of the clock mechanic like any attack. The orbs fade when the clock intermission or the color intermission starts (`BouncingOrb.DespawnAll()` through `ClearLingeringHazards`).
- Harness with the second boss: key 5 forces it (`DebugForceOrbBarrage`); F5 can repeat it alone through `Attack To Use`.

**Verified:** compiles with 0 console errors; forcing it spawns 3 orbs with a ground footprint each. Checked in the CLI playtest (2026-10-06): the footprint circle meets the wall line on the left, right and top walls (`Orb Wall Inset` looks right by eye; the bottom wall was not looked at), a head-on orb-orb hit deflects both and each keeps its speed, an orb on the player costs 1 heart, the scene stays clean. **Not verified yet:** the bottom wall, the despawn at the intermission, a natural run mixed with the other attacks, and balance (speed 12.5 (the first guess was 7; raised by 30%, 25% and then 10% after playtests that felt too easy), the bounce bonus 45-65% (it was 20-40%), and lifetime 9 are guesses, only a hand playtest can judge them).

**Look:** the trail is a thin soft purple tail (`OrbTrail` material with the generated gradient `OrbTrailSoft.png`, width 0.6, 0.45 s, fading out). The wall and orb-orb bounce effect is `OrbBounceEffect`, a copy of the Hovl `Electro hit` with its start colors changed to purple (the pack materials are HDR, so a plain purple still renders bright).

**Known limits / ideas:** no sound; the cast animation is the same placeholder as the spell circles; the orbs do not bounce off the player or the boss; no phase 2 variant. `DebugForceOrbBarrage` does nothing while the boss is busy, so with auto attacks on it may need a second try.
