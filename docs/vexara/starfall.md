# Vexara: Starfall

The fourth attack of the wizard (after Spell Circles, Diagonal Lines and Orb Barrage). First version, meant to be iterated after playtesting. Branch `feature/starfall`.

**What it does:** the boss raises both hands (the same `Cast Area` animation as the clock waves, through `PlayCastArea`) and calls 4 stars down, one every 0.4 s. An 8-pointed star marked in red on the floor (the body of the star is the damage zone, radius 1.2 m, and the points, 2.5 m from the center, face the 8 laser directions) fills from the center out over 2.2 s (`IndicatorBase` is the dim full-size star, `IndicatorFill` the brighter copy that grows from 0 to full size; texture `StarIndicator.png`, materials `StarIndicatorBase` and `StarIndicatorFill`) while the star, a small 8-pointed star, falls at a steady speed from 14 m up and lands as the circle is full, so the player can see it coming and walk away. On landing it hurts everything inside the body of the star (radius 1.2 m plus a 0.2 m padding) for 1 and shoots 8 lasers along the floor with no warning, one per point of the star (up, down, left, right and the four diagonals). Each laser is a bolt 5 m long and 0.6 m wide that flies out of the impact point at 14 m/s up to the arena edge, with the tail following the head so the part behind disappears; it hurts anywhere along the bolt for 1 (the player's own hit immunity stops repeated hits). The 8-point shape of the falling star is the only hint of the burst, on purpose, so players learn it. After the last star the boss waits 0.5 s and teleports like after the other attacks, and the attack ends one `timeBetweenAttacks` (0.35 s) before the last star lands, so the next attack starts casting as it lands and not while stars are still falling (measured: the boss was free 0.35 s before the last landing; the lasers of the last stars are still flying then). Stars and lasers keep going into the next attack, but the clock intermission and the color intermission remove them when they start (`ClearLingeringHazards`).

**Where the stars land:** at least `Star Edge Margin` 3 m from every edge of the arena, so the whole star on the floor is inside the map (checked over 60 simulated attacks: never closer than 3.0 m); the stars are spread out. Each one either aims near the player like a spell circle (`Star Near Player Chance` 0.4, the same lead or random offset as the circles) or lands at a random spot up to `Star Max Player Distance` 15 m from the player, and a candidate closer than `Star Min Spacing` 7 m to a star already placed is rejected (up to `Star Placement Attempts` 25 tries, then the best one). Measured over 40 simulated attacks: the closest two stars were never nearer than 7 m, 8.2 m on average.

**Pieces**
- `Bosses/Vexara/StarfallStar.cs` (prefab `StarfallStar`): one star. Owns its timer, draws the star indicator, moves the `Visual` (the star sprite, scale 0.6, with a trail and a point light) down in a straight line, spins it facing the camera, and on landing plays `SpellCircleImpact` (scaled by `Impact Effect Scale` 0.7), does the circle damage and spawns the 8 lasers. `StarfallSettings` (serializable, in the same file) holds radius, telegraph time, damage, laser speed, width, length and damage.
- `Bosses/Vexara/StarLaser.cs` (prefab `StarLaser`): one bolt. Position-based like the orbs: a flat quad on the floor (material `LaserBeam`, additive, texture `LaserBeam.png` with a bright core and soft edges) between the tail and the head, which both move at the laser speed; damage is a distance test against the segment from the tail to the head (`width / 2 + Player Hit Padding` across).
- Sprite `Materials/StarIcon.png`: the 8-pointed star, generated in code (outer radius 118 px, inner 50 px, white-gold core fading to violet).
- `VexaraBoss.cs`: `Starfall` header (`Star Prefab`, `Star Count` 4, `Star Cast Delay` 0.5, `Star Interval` 0.4, `Star After Time` 0.5, the placement fields above and the `Starfall` settings), `StarfallSequence`, `GetStarCenter`; `VexaraAttack.Starfall` makes it part of the random attack pool (never twice in a row) and of the clock mechanic like any attack.
- Harness with Vexara: key 7 forces it (`DebugForceStarfall`); F5 can repeat it alone through `Attack To Use`.

**History of this skill:** the first version burst into 8 small star projectiles; they were replaced by a line of stars per direction then by lasers that grew from the impact point (1 m wide, the whole length on the floor), and finally by moving bolts, because the player could slip between the projectiles and the idea is that the burst after the circle is the main danger.

**Verified:** compiles with 0 console errors; forced in play, the circles appear, the star falls and lands as the circle fills, 8 bolts fly out in the 8 directions with the tail following the head, the arrows show around the circle, and a bolt flying over the player cost 1 heart (a bolt 3 m to the side did not). **Not verified yet:** the speed and width balance (laser speed 14 m/s leaves about 0.1 s to react once the star lands, so the dodge is meant to happen during the fall), the look of the star, the arrows, the bolts and the smaller impact effect on a real screen, a natural run mixed with the other attacks and the clock mechanic.

**Known limits / ideas:** no sound; no phase 2 variant; the lasers ignore walls, orbs and each other (they simply stop at the arena edge); the cast animation is the shared area cast.
