# Vexara: Phase 2

Starts when the color intermission ends (see `color-intermission.md`). The phase flag (`hasEnteredSecondPhase`, `IsInSecondPhase`) is set as soon as the boss drops to half health, and every phase 2 change below is read from that flag (harness F3 toggles the flag, so it can be tested without playing the intermission). Branch `feature/phase-two`.

**Power up:** when the last color round is over the boss plays `Standing 2H Magic Attack 05` (3.5 s, Humanoid with the boss avatar copied; state `Power Up` of the Vexara Controller, `Power Up Time` 3.5 s): it crouches low and bursts upward with both arms thrown open. It stays immune, then teleports away like after every attack. (A first version used Gorath's `Standing Yell`, which looked childish, and was dropped.)

**Intermission facing:** during the whole color intermission the boss stands in the middle looking straight ahead (toward the camera) and does not follow the player. It uses plain waits instead of `WaitFacingPlayer`.

**What changed in phase 2 (all other values stay the same)**
- **Clock mechanic: off.** No clock cast, no numbers, no clock intermission (`clockMechanicEnabled` is false once the flag is set).
- **Spell Circles (Karthus Q): two circles instead of one at each step.** Same circle damage (hurts inside, the player dodges), radius (1.2 m), interval (0.42 s) and telegraph (0.5 s). Each step picks the usual aimed spot and puts 2 circles on opposite sides of it (`Phase Two Circles Per Step` 2), `Phase Two Circle Distance` 2.4 m from the spot, which leaves a safe corridor about 1.2 m wide in the middle: the player either stands in the middle or runs away. There are `Phase Two Circle Steps` 4 steps (8 circles) instead of 7. (Three circles, 120 degrees apart, were tried first and were too difficult.) A first version made the circles safe zones and flashed the whole arena outside them, which felt epileptic and was removed.
- **Timed Explosions ("memory big attack"): 6 steps, no safe spot, vertical and horizontal mixed, and not twice close together.** The zones are the same size as in phase 1 (thirds of the arena). The sequence has `Phase Two Zone Steps` 6 steps: a full set of three thirds (all vertical or all horizontal, so every spot of the arena is hit at least once and the player has to dodge for sure) plus three extra steps picked from the thirds of both directions, the first one always from the other direction, so the sequence always mixes vertical and horizontal zones and a zone can come up twice. The steps are shuffled. Same light time (0.5 s, 0.2 s apart) and explosion interval (1.3 s). Simulated over 300 sequences with 5 steps: all mixed both directions and left no spot uncovered (the 6-step version uses the same rule). In phase 2 the attack also has a cooldown: after Timed Explosions at least `Phase Two Zone Cooldown` 2 other attacks have to be cast before it can come up again (`_attacksSinceZones`), **and none of its zones may still be on the field** (`IsExplosionZonesOnCooldown`). The attack hands the boss back at the first explosion, so two short spells could end while the 6 explosions were still going and a second memory game started on top of the first; the zone check closes that gap. Checked in play: with 9 spells since but zones alive the attack was never picked (0 of 1500), once the zones were gone it was picked again (329 of 1500), and with only 1 spell since it was never picked. The cooldown does not apply in phase 1.
- **Orb Barrage: 4 orbs instead of 3** (`Phase Two Orb Count` 4; 5 was too much), thrown 0.7 s apart over the same 25 degree cone on each side of the player (about 17 degrees between them).
- **Diagonal Lines: 30% faster.** One set, as in phase 1, but the whole attack runs 1.3 times faster (`Phase Two Line Speed`): the wind-up, the warning (1.17 s becomes 0.90 s, measured), the delay before the boss teleports and the pause after the blast are all divided by 1.3. The cast animation itself is not sped up. Phase 1 is untouched (speed 1). Three sets in a row, one after the other and then almost on top of each other, were tried and were too hard.

**Not changed:** Starfall and the teleport. (The teleport is not an attack: it is the vanish and reappear the boss does at the end of most attacks, and harness key 2 only forces it for testing.)

**Verified:** compiles with 0 console errors; with the phase flag on, Spell Circles spawned 2 circles in the first step, Timed Explosions sequences (simulated) have mixed steps and no uncovered spot, the zones cooldown held in simulation, diagonal lines warn 0.90 s in phase 2 and 1.17 s in phase 1, Orb Barrage spawns 4 orbs; the intermission ran to the end, the boss faced forward the whole time and the `Power Up` state played. **Not verified yet:** how the power up looks in motion (only checked the pose frames of the clip), how it plays by hand: the size of the safe middle between the circle pairs (1.2 m), whether 6 explosion steps are too long, a natural run of phase 2 mixed with the other attacks.

**Ideas:** a shockwave or screen shake on the power up; a boss tint in phase 2; phase 2 variants for Starfall.
