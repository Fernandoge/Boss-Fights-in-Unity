# Ideas

A parking lot for ideas. Add them in a line or two, don't design yet. When one is picked up, move it to **In progress** and link its design note under `docs/`.

Format: `- #N Idea name [size]: what it is and why. (needs: assets, decisions or tech)`. Size is S (under an hour), M (a session), L (several sessions). Open and in-progress ideas are numbered from 1 (renumbered on 2026-10-11 and kept fixed from now on). The numbers under **Done or dropped** are the old ones from the first brain dump (2026-10-08), so they overlap with the open numbers: say "done #2" when you mean those.

- #12 and #13 (open numbers) Axeman Q and R tweaks, 2026-10-12: the Q channel now loops only the gather of the battlecry (0.05 to 0.45 s of the clip, found by sampling the pose: the head goes back and the roar starts at about 0.45 s) and the scream plays from 0.45 s only on the tenth press. The repeated explosions after the R landing were the pack effects (`Stones hit`, `Star hit`) having Looping on, which repeated the burst every second until the 3 s lifetime ended; `AxemanController.PlayOnce` turns looping off on every spawned effect (also the hit impact). Checked in Play mode through the CLI (no looping hit effects after the leap, the Q scream plays after 10 presses, 0 errors); not judged by eye: how the new gather loop looks while mashing.

- #5 (open #5) Axeman and ninja tweak pass, 2026-10-11 (second round done after a playtest): the ninja's dash is 8.5 m (was 10). The Axeman's Space dash is a real forward jump (`Unarmed Jump Forward`, about 6 m and 0.75 m high, measured; the dive roll it used before read as sliding, not jumping) and it no longer gives any invulnerability, so it is worth using for the distance and the animation cancel only. R is now one press, the Execution Leap: no Battlecry or rage any more, 11.44 m at most (the 14.3 m of the first round was too much), 12 s cooldown, and it rises with the distance (`Arc Height Factor` 0.6, peak about 3.4 m measured) so it reads as a jump and not a glide. E is a new Shield Charge: a 4 m low shoulder charge like a rugby tackle (run clip, body pitched 50 degrees, stops in front of an enemy) with a 0.5 s shield bubble (the ninja's `DodgeBubble`, he cannot be hurt while it lasts), 7 s cooldown, cancellable after about 0.2 s. The Axe Throw moved to D (temporary). Counter key F for both characters and the phase 2 immunity fix (bosses cannot lose health between the half-health hit and the end of the intermission, `EndSecondPhaseTransition`) were checked and approved. Not checked by eye: the dive roll, the shape of the tackle from the real camera. Later the same day: every basic hit does 2 (the third used to do 3), the R landing bursts a ring across the floor as wide as the 4.5 m it damages (`Shared/GroundSplash`, built in code) so it reads as an area hit, and the E charge straightens up over about 0.35 s instead of snapping back to the stance the moment the move ends. Design and numbers: `docs/axeman/design.md`.
